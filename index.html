The problem

A map pin never told anyone where the power outlet is.

Scouting falls apart between the moodboard and the call sheet. The location looked perfect in someone's saved folder — then the gate is locked, the light is gone by four, and there is nowhere to park the van.

CityFrame keeps the visual reference and the production reality in the same record, so the decision you make at your desk still holds on the day.

Built for the shoot day

Choose a location with the call sheet already in mind.

  • Search by creative intent

    Filter for rooftops, brutalist texture, golden hour, water reflections, or whatever is walkable from where you stand.

  • Validate the practical details

    Power, parking, restrooms, accessibility, public access hours, and permit notes — recorded per location, not guessed.

  • Save and share what matters

    Organise spots into collections, hand them to the crew with a link, and keep the best ideas ready for the next production.

The workflow

From first idea to call time.

Discovery, logistics, and planning sit in one flow — so the shoot does not fall apart the moment the location is picked.

01

03
